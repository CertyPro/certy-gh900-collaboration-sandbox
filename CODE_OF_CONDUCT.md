# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this sandbox to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone
learning GitHub, regardless of experience, background, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient, and encouraging, especially to first-time contributors.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respecting that everyone is here to learn.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Our responsibilities

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull
requests that do not align with this code of conduct, and may block repeat or
serious offenders.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, and discussions.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it privately to
the maintainers via the **Security** tab's private advisory, or by the contact
listed in `SECURITY.md` where available. Reports will be handled discreetly.
